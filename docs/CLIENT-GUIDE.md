# Olea Social website: how to edit it

Sign in at **https://cms.oleasocial.co.uk/wp-admin** with your own username and password.

This is where the website's words, prices, photos and PDFs live. When you click **Update**, the website
(https://oleasocial.co.uk) shows the change within a few seconds. Refresh the page to see it. If it hasn't shown after a minute, save again or contact Doqtra.

Visiting cms.oleasocial.co.uk without `/wp-admin` just sends you to the website. That's normal.

## Menus (left sidebar → Menus)

Each menu is one tab on the website's Menu page (A la carte, Lunch, Bar, Wine List …).

- **Change a price or dish:** open the menu → scroll to the section → edit the dish's **Name**, **Description**
  or **Price** → **Update**.
  - Price: number only, no £, written like the other dishes (e.g. `9.5` or `14`). It shows exactly as you
    type it. Leave it blank on set menus.
  - Put dietary tags in the description, e.g. `(V)` or `(VG)`.
- **Add a dish:** in the section's Dishes table, click **Add Row**. Drag the ≡ handle to reorder.
- **Sections:** each section has a **Title**, an optional **Note** (small line under the heading) and **Featured**
  (highlights the section).
- **Menu price / intro:** **Price** (e.g. `£19.50 per person`) and **Intro** sit at the top of the menu.
- **Menu options:** for menus with several priced options side by side (e.g. Christmas). Leave empty otherwise.
- **PDF download:** click **Remove**, then **Add File** to upload the new PDF. The website's download link updates by itself.
- **Order of the tabs:** the **Order** number in the right-hand box (lower numbers come first).
- The title is the tab name. Don't change the **slug** unless you mean to: it's part of links like `/menu#a-la-carte`.

## What's On (left sidebar → What's On)

- **Add an event or offer:** **Add What's On item** → title, **Category** (Festive, Menus or Events), **Date**
  (shown exactly as you type it, e.g. `7 November 2026 · 19:30`), **Summary**, **Image** → **Publish**.
- **Last day:** optional. The day after it, the item moves to Past events by itself.
- **Show on home page:** tick it to feature the item on the home page.
- Optional: **Banner** (wide picture on the event page), **Details** (extra paragraphs), **Linked menu** (shows
  that menu's price and a link to it).
- To take something down, **Move to Trash**.
- The heading and intro of the What's On page itself are under **Olea Site → What's On page**.

## Blog (left sidebar → Posts)

**Add New Post** → title and text → set the **Featured image** (right-hand side; it's the post's photo) →
**Publish**. To edit, open the post and **Update**.

## Photos and alt text

Upload under **Media → Add New**. Click a photo and fill in **Alternative Text** (a short description for people
who can't see it). The website uses that text wherever the photo appears.

## Site settings (left sidebar → Olea Site)

- **Site settings:** name, tagline, address, phone, email, booking link, map link, dietary note, opening
  hours (Days + Time rows) and social links.
- **Home page:** intro text and the promo panels (eyebrow, title, text, button label + link, image).
- **About page:** story, about the name, regions (one per line), slideshow and gallery photos.
- **Private dining page:** the intro text.
- **What's On page:** heading and intro.

In long text boxes, leave an empty line between paragraphs.

## Please don't

- Change anything under **Settings**, **Plugins**, **Appearance** or **Users** (other than your own profile).
  They're set up for the website and changing them can break it.
- Untick **Settings → Reading → Discourage search engines**. Google should index only the real website.
